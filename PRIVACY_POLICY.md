# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

This Privacy Policy explains what data the **Storyteller Scehduler** Discord
bot ("the Bot") collects, how it's used, and how it's stored.

## Who operates this Bot

The Bot is self-hosted and operated by the administrator(s) of the Discord
server(s) it's installed in ("the Operator"). It is not a publicly listed or
centrally-run service — it runs on infrastructure the Operator controls.

## What data the Bot collects

When you interact with the Bot — for example, by holding a role it's
configured to track, responding to its direct messages, or reacting to its
messages — it may store:

- Your Discord user ID
- Your position in the sign-up queue
- Which slot(s), if any, you're assigned to in a given round
- Whether you've asked to be skipped ("snoozed") and until when
- The server, channel, and role IDs the Bot is configured to use

The Bot does **not** collect or store:

- Message content from any channel — it does not read chat messages
- Your email address, IP address, or anything outside Discord
- Payment or financial information
- Data from any server the Bot is not installed in

## How data is used

Data is used solely to operate the sign-up queue: determining whose turn it
is, direct messaging you to ask if you'd like to sign up, tracking slot
assignments, and posting results in the configured channel.

## How data is stored

All data is stored locally, in a single file, on infrastructure controlled
by the Operator — it is never sent to a third party, an analytics service,
or an advertiser.

## Data retention and removal

- Your user ID is removed from the queue automatically the next time an
  admin runs `/queue-sync`, if you no longer hold the tracked role.
- An admin can adjust or remove your data at any time — for example via
  `/queue-move` or `/polling-edit`, or by editing the underlying data file
  directly.
- To request removal of your data, contact a server administrator.

## Children's privacy

Using the Bot requires a Discord account, which under Discord's own Terms
of Service requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. The
Bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone who doesn't meet that
requirement.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot
after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the
administrators of the Discord server where the Bot is installed.
